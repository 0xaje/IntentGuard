01

Policy commitment

The user’s versioned policy hash establishes the authorized limits without placing raw intent text on-chain.

02

Evaluator signature

An authorized evaluator signs the EIP-712 receipt; the signature is bound to the receipt fields and chain.

03

Evidence hashes

Intent, request, and evidence hashes make the exact decision inputs tamper-evident and reproducible.

04

Base Sepolia anchor

The receipt is anchored with expiry and revocation state—without custody, asset transfers, or arbitrary calls.

Trust is inspectable: receipt contracts record commitments and proof, not funds—and expired or revoked receipts are never presented as current.
