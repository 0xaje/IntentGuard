06 · Trust boundary

Safety is a product feature—
and a trust boundary.

IntentGuard earns trust by refusing powers it does not need. The MVP evaluates a proposed action, explains its evidence, and leaves signing and custody with the user.
What IntentGuard does

Read-only

Analyzes calldata, typed data, and Base evidence without moving funds.

Non-custodial

Never requests seed phrases, private keys, or account control.

Fail closed

Unknown selectors and missing critical evidence become CANNOT VERIFY.

Evidence-led

Policy rules and versioned receipts make every verdict inspectable.

What it never does

No custody.
No arbitrary execution.

  • Never signs or broadcasts a user transaction.
  • Never holds user assets in the registry contracts.
  • Never turns incomplete evidence into a “safe” label.
Honest scope

An external EOA can still sign elsewhere. IntentGuard protects the approval flow it is integrated into; universal enforcement requires a programmable account or wallet integration, which is explicitly outside this MVP.
